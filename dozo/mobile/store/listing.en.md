## Description [4000]

Dozo gives you bots that stay around. Each one keeps its conversation, its memory and its
routines, and works on a computer of its own. You talk to them like teammates, alone or in a group,
and they ask before they do anything that matters.

- Chat with a bot, or put several in a group and give them one request
- Approve or refuse what a bot wants to do before it happens
- Routines that run a bot on a schedule
- Memory a bot reads and keeps up to date between chats
- Voice: dictate a message, or call a bot
- Attach photos and files, or share them into Dozo from another app
- Use Dozo's hosted service, your own server, or your own computer as the server, paired by
  scanning a code it shows

Sign in with your account on the hosted service, or open the app on a server you run yourself.
Bots use the model connection you set up. Dozo's source code is available at
https://github.com/SSilistre-dev/dozo.

Dozo is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any other app it builds on.
